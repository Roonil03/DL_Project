# Indian sector study review — 15 November 2026

The completed `india-study` run does not establish that a neural architecture
improves on the simple strategies tested here. At 5 basis points per unit of
turnover, DLinear has the highest average neural Sharpe, 0.3801, but its mean
changes from 1.4300 in 2020 to -0.6698 in 2023. The long/cash trend baseline
has Sharpe 2.1712 and 2.4922 respectively and exceeds every neural seed in both
folds. This is a descriptive comparison of two selected years, not a claim of
statistical significance or live investment performance.

## Completion and provenance

DLinear, LSTM, genuine Mamba and compact PatchTST trained for seeds 42, 52, 62,
72 and 82 across two folds: 40 completed units. All 44 saved notebooks have no
error outputs: 40 benchmark notebooks plus core, data audit, validation and
reporting. Validation records 58 tests passed and 4 skipped, with 5 warnings.
These checks establish tested behavior, not freedom from every possible leakage
or implementation error. No optional AHAT-Fin or MoE experiment was run.

The final archive is `artifacts/india-study.zip`, 86,236,936 bytes.
It contains 868 manifest-listed files plus the manifest, including 120 checkpoint
files and 40 completion markers. ZIP CRC checks and every manifest member's size
and SHA-256 passed. All 868 files were also extracted and their hashes verified.
Raw market inputs are excluded; `code/src/data/` contains loader source code.

There were no recorded training-unit failures or retries. Later validation
and packaging needed runtime repairs and resumed completed units;
the 40 models were not retrained. Intermediate failed states are not final
completion evidence. Review final state, saved notebooks, hashes and events together.

Use the archived source, dependency lock, configuration and `metadata/identity.json`
file hashes as well: the revision alone does not identify later runtime repairs.
Do not describe this as a retraining at a newer commit. The image tag is not an
immutable image digest.

## Data and evaluation dates

NIFTY BANK, NIFTY IT and NIFTY
PHARMA have 3,675 aligned complete Close-price sessions from 31 January 2011 to
31 December 2025. Returns are decimal close-to-close price returns, excluding
dividends. These indices are not directly tradable funds. No macro series is
used. The [data manifest](runs/india-study/data_manifest.json) records source
details and hashes.

The fixed 2025 cutoff avoids missing source sessions in 2026. Partial missingness
is rejected without forward filling. Hashes identify bytes, not independently
verified vendor accuracy or historical vintages. Retrieval time is unrecorded.
Underlying provider rights remain unverified; obtain raw data separately under
the source's terms. This report does not grant redistribution rights.

- Fold 0 trains in 2014–2018, validates in 2019 and tests in 2020: 249 decisions
  from 2020-01-01 to 2020-12-30, realized targets 2020-01-02 to 2020-12-31,
  and 747 asset/date signal observations.
- Fold 1 trains in 2017–2021, validates in 2022 and tests in 2023: 244 decisions
  from 2023-01-02 to 2023-12-28, targets 2023-01-03 to 2023-12-29, and 732
  asset/date signal observations.

These India calendar folds control evaluation. Legacy `data.yaml` calendar fields
and `backtest.yaml` index-based defaults also appear in the snapshot but do not
define this run's test dates. Assets on the same day and repeated seeds are
dependent; folds share training history. Ten units are not ten independent market
experiments, and the entire 2011–2025 panel was not tested.

Features and sizing volatility are shifted by one observed session. Scaling and
regime thresholds use training data, and realized target dates follow decisions.
Actual close-time execution prices, slippage, financing, short availability and
a tradable index implementation remain unverified. Source-vintage, dataset
selection and repeated research choices can still bias conclusions. Earlier
test years may enter a later fold's training history; research choices informed
by these results require a new untouched test period for confirmation.

## Results and baselines

The [generated report](../Final_Metrics.md) is preserved verbatim. Mean Sharpe is
the arithmetic average of ten separately evaluated seed/fold Sharpes, not the
Sharpe of a concatenated strategy or ensemble. SD Sharpe is the sample standard
deviation across those units: it mixes seed and year variation and is neither
a confidence interval nor a standard error.

At 5 bps, mean neural Sharpes (those SDs) are DLinear 0.3801 (1.2975), Mamba
-0.1809 (1.0148), LSTM -0.2994 (1.1678), and PatchTST -0.6360 (0.6702).
Their 2020 / 2023 means are respectively 1.4300 / -0.6698, 0.4208 / -0.7827,
0.3720 / -0.9709, and -0.9740 / -0.2980. Positive net Sharpe occurs in 7/10,
6/10, 4/10 and 3/10 units respectively. This variation prevents a stable
architecture conclusion.

Baseline names describe different exposures:

- `baseline_equal_weight` offsets common volatility scaling to give unit
  exposure per asset, averaged across assets. Mean Sharpe is 1.7026.
- `baseline_long_only` goes long when the preceding 20-session return sum is
  positive, otherwise cash, then applies common volatility sizing. Mean Sharpe
  is 2.3317.
- `baseline_momentum` uses the sign of that trailing sum, permitting shorts;
  mean Sharpe is 1.5186.
- `baseline_volatility_managed` supplies +1 signals before common volatility
  sizing; mean Sharpe is 1.7957.
- `baseline_zero` is flat. Zero Sharpe is the code convention for zero volatility,
  not an estimated investment ratio.

Baselines are deterministic, saved under every requested seed for traceability.
Those copies do not increase sample size. Their reported SD reflects two years
repeated five times, not stochastic training uncertainty.

Costs are a single linear charge on absolute position changes at 0, 1, 5, 10
or 20 bps. Annualized turnover is exposure turnover per year, not a percentage.
DLinear's mean Sharpe drops from 0.9701 at zero cost to 0.3801 at 5 bps,
-0.2086 at 10 bps and -1.3762 at 20 bps. Other neural means are already negative
at zero cost. Lower drawdown or volatility may reflect lower exposure; a 10%
sizing target does not guarantee 10% realized portfolio volatility. Every neural
unit records zero leverage-cap fraction.

Signals are bounded trading preferences optimized with portfolio Sharpe loss.
MSE compares those signals directly with decimal next-session returns; it is
not calibrated return-forecast error. Directional accuracy pools asset/date pairs,
not independent days. Baseline signals have different scales, so their MSEs must
not be ranked as competing forecasts. Flat signals have small MSE because daily
returns are small, while conveying no directional information.

## Regimes and uncertainty

Distinct 2020 decision-date counts are Extreme Stress 183, HighVol Bear 3,
HighVol Bull 20, LowVol Bear 2 and LowVol Bull 41. In 2023 only LowVol Bear 51
and LowVol Bull 193 occur. Stress is a rule label using training thresholds,
not an external dated crisis annotation. Two or three days cannot establish a
robust annualized regime ranking. Absent regimes are unavailable, not zero.
The generated aggregate-regime table sums seed repetitions: its 915 stress
observations are 183 dates repeated five times. Per-unit counts are correct.

Per-unit regime intervals use 1,000 resamples of fixed 20-observation moving
blocks with 2.5th/97.5th percentiles. Despite an older helper docstring, this is
not a stationary bootstrap. Fewer than 40 selected observations produce no CI.
Filtering a regime before forming blocks can join disjoint calendar dates,
limiting dependence interpretation. Intervals condition on one trained strategy
and selected dates and have no multiple-comparison adjustment. For example,
DLinear seed 42's 2020 stress Sharpe is 1.5589, CI [-0.7324, 4.1322].

No persisted paired architecture-comparison or adjusted significance result was
found. Aggregate SDs are not substitutes. Statistical neural superiority or
general crisis robustness is not established.

`crisis_windows.csv` selects each strategy's worst gross rolling 63-session
window after seeing its test results. Dates differ across strategies; this is
neither a matched-date crisis comparison nor a crisis forecast. The summary
equity/drawdown PNG uses gross returns from seed 42, fold 0 only; it is not the
net five-seed, two-fold aggregate.

## Identity, tuning and compute

DLinear is DLinear-inspired; PatchTST is a compact patch Transformer. Results
describe repository implementations, not exact paper reproductions. Mamba uses
`mamba-ssm` 2.2.4 with `allow_fallback=false`, constructing genuine Mamba blocks.
The CPU interface fallback is not the benchmark path.

Each unit compares two preset configurations for three tuning epochs using
negative validation Sharpe, then trains the selected configuration for at most
100 epochs with patience 10. The selection record says test data was unused.
Actual epochs are DLinear 11–62, LSTM 11–33, Mamba 11–61 and PatchTST 11–22.
This is a small bounded search, not exhaustive equal-capacity tuning. Selected
parameter counts vary: DLinear 99; LSTM 6,755 or 54,979; Mamba 10,627 or 66,691;
PatchTST 15,171 or 106,819. Family means mix selected capacities. All histories
report zero skipped batches.

The environment is Python 3.11.11, PyTorch 2.4.1+cu124, CUDA 12.4 and an NVIDIA
GeForce RTX 3060 Laptop GPU. The 40 units ran from 06:50:19 to 07:00:52 UTC,
about 10 minutes 33 seconds. Unit durations sum to 631 seconds, median 15 and
P95 24 seconds, including notebook overhead. Training-loop means are about
4.05 / 4.34 / 6.04 / 5.27 seconds for DLinear / LSTM / Mamba / PatchTST,
excluding much orchestration and tuning. Repair and packaging time are additional.

Across 762 epoch events, peak PyTorch allocation is 32,223,744 bytes (30.7 MiB);
sampled total device use peaks at 844,808,192 bytes (805.7 MiB). Sampled GPU
utilization averages 18.6% and peaks at 55%. These sparse observations may
include other processes and do not establish exclusive utilization or deployment
throughput.
